ShowFlix Release Radar: Most Anticipated TV & Film Premieres (2025/2026)

By ShowFlix Editorial • Curated Season Radar • 7 Min Read

Following extensive Hollywood production delays and studio shifts, 2025 and 2026 are primed to deliver one of the most concentrated releases of prestige television finales, returning cult favorites, and massive theatrical epics in history. Here is your definitive editorial breakdown of what to add to your ShowFlix radar.

1. Flagship Returning Television Series

Stranger Things (Season 5 — The Final Season)

Netflix • Sci-Fi / Supernatural Drama
Netflix Target: Mid 2025

The culmination of nearly a decade of 80s nostalgia and Upside Down mythology. The Duffer Brothers have confirmed that Season 5 will waste no time resetting the board, picking up immediately in the aftermath of Hawkins splitting apart. With feature-length episode runtimes and major emotional stakes, it stands as the year's definitive streaming event.

Severance (Season 2)

Apple TV+ • Dystopian Psychological Mystery
Apple TV+ Airing Now

Following one of television's most celebrated cliffhangers in recent memory, Mark Scout and the Macrodata Refinement division face the consequences of their outside-world rebellion. Helmed by Ben Stiller and creator Dan Erickson, Season 2 expands Lumon Industries' chilling corporate apparatus.

The Last of Us (Season 2)

HBO / Max • Post-Apocalyptic Drama
HBO Max Spring 2025

Craig Mazin and Neil Druckmann adapt the critically revered and emotionally brutal events of The Last of Us Part II. Pedro Pascal and Bella Ramsey return alongside Kaitlyn Dever as Abby, promising a morally complex examination of grief and retribution.

The Bear (Season 4)

FX on Hulu / Disney+ • Drama / Comedy
Disney+ June 2025

Filmed largely back-to-back with Season 3, Christopher Storer's Chicago culinary pressure cooker returns to answer whether Carmy's relentless pursuit of perfection will destroy the chosen family he fought to assemble.

2. Cinematic Blockbusters & Theatrical Radars

  • Superman (July 2025): James Gunn inaugurates the revitalized DC Universe with David Corenswet embodying the Man of Steel.
  • Fantastic Four: First Steps (July 2025): Marvel Studios welcomes Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Joseph Quinn, and Ebon Moss-Bachrach into a retro-futuristic 1960s aesthetic.
  • Avatar: Fire and Ash (December 2025): James Cameron continues his Pandora journey, introducing the antagonistic volcanic Ash People.
