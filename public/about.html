About ShowFlix

ShowFlix was born out of a simple frustration: entertainment apps have become cluttered, closed, and increasingly monetized with aggressive lock-ins. We set out to build a modern, high-performance media tracker that gives complete power back to the user.

Our Core Philosophy: Open Data Sovereignty

Most media tracking services store your library on closed, private servers. If you want to export your data or switch platforms, you often encounter paywalls, data loss, or incompatible formats.

At ShowFlix, we believe your viewing history belongs entirely to you. That is why ShowFlix operates on a revolutionary two-way architecture directly synced with your personal Google Drive spreadsheet:

100% Data Ownership

Every title, rating, season progress, and note lives inside your own personal Google Sheet. You can edit it from any device, export it to CSV, or back it up indefinitely.

Zero Account Lock-in

No mandatory proprietary login walls or paywalls. Simply connect your Google Sheet URL, and your library renders in a gorgeous cinema-style visual UI.

Offline-Ready Resilience

Your library is cached locally in your browser. Update episode counts on an airplane or subway, and ShowFlix automatically synchronizes back to your Google Sheet when back online.

Editorial Independence & Transparency

Our publishing team produces independent entertainment guides, streaming service comparisons, and release radar calendars. We evaluate series and films on artistic merit, storytelling quality, and viewer value — never influenced by corporate studio affiliations.

Built for Enthusiasts, Maintained with Pride

ShowFlix is actively maintained by entertainment enthusiasts. We are dedicated to providing a fast, ad-supported, accessible utility that respects your attention and celebrates the art of cinema and television.
