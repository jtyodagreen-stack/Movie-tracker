Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At ShowFlix (accessible from https://show-flix.co.uk), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by ShowFlix and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

ShowFlix is designed as a client-first, private personal media tracker:

  • Local Browser Storage: Your tracker preferences, theme settings, filters, and offline queue are stored locally on your device in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB cache.
  • Google Sheets Sync (Optional): When you choose to sync with Google Sheets, ShowFlix uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access only the specific spreadsheet you configure. We do not store your spreadsheet data on external central servers; your tracker communicates directly between your browser and the official Google Sheets API.
  • Public Showcase Catalog: Visitors browsing our public featured catalog can view show details, trailers, and release countdowns without providing any personal information.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Third-Party Advertising Policy:

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to show-flix.co.uk and other sites on the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Log Files and Web Beacons

ShowFlix follows standard procedures of using log files and standard web metrics. These logs include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. GDPR and CCPA Privacy Rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you are entitled to full data sovereignty:

  • The right to access, rectify, or erase any personal information stored in your local tracker.
  • The right to disconnect Google Sheets or remove authorization at any time via your Google Account security permissions.
  • The right to opt-out of personal data collection or tracking cookies through your browser settings or privacy preference tools.
